# SYNTHETIC LapseForecaster brain-content ablation

**SYNTHETIC DATA. Not a result.** Sensor streams, SART trials and experience-sampling answers are
generated by `ml/forecast/synth_forecast.py`; content timelines are MOCK. Lapse onsets were *made*
to depend on MOCK drift risk / valley proximity, so this only shows the pipeline works and that
content features can be used when such a coupling exists. Report real-data numbers only.

Test set: 25 held-out subjects (subject-wise split), 656 lapse onsets. Alert rate fixed at 10% of scored seconds. Chance precision = positive rate = 0.291. Seeds: [0, 1, 2] (mean ± sd).

| Variant | AUROC | Precision @ 10% alert rate | Median lead time (s) | Onsets warned (≤15 s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SYNTHETIC with content | 0.726 ± 0.003 | 0.651 ± 0.010 | 13.5 ± 0.3 | 0.47 ± 0.01 |
| SYNTHETIC without content | 0.567 ± 0.004 | 0.414 ± 0.019 | 8.9 ± 0.2 | 0.56 ± 0.00 |
| SYNTHETIC previous model with content | 0.613 | 0.388 | 10.9 | 0.40 |
| SYNTHETIC previous model without content | 0.525 | 0.289 | 12.1 | 0.22 |

| SYNTHETIC heuristic without content | 0.519 ± 0.000 | 0.296 ± 0.000 | 9.3 ± 0.0 | 0.35 ± 0.00 |

Lead time = median seconds between the first alert in the 15 s before an onset and the onset (detected onsets only). Heuristic rows score `HeuristicLapseForecaster` default weights (Python mirror).

"previous model" rows score the Core ML models shipped before Phase 4 (trained on the older, simpler SYNTHETIC generator) on the same held-out test subjects; they are not retrained per seed.

Training also used train-only sessions from the legacy SYNTHETIC generator (`ml/forecast/out/legacy/manifest.csv`); validation and test subjects come only from the realistic generator.
